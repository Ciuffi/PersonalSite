At DubHacks 2019, a hackathon in Seattle, my team and I created a magnification app to
help people with low vision. It used **machine learning** to enhance pictures taken
with the camera and read text aloud using **Google's optical character recognition**.

I teamed up with two people I'd just met, and we placed **second for the community
track**, **second for the Google Cloud prize**, and **second for the AI incubator
prize**.

Built with:

- Expo / React Native
- Google ML Vision and OCR
- Google Firebase Functions
- Google Firestore
