StarChaser is a simple but addictive Apple Watch game developed for the **watchOS 6
SDK** to run as an independent app — no iPhone required. It's built entirely with
Xcode and Swift, and shipped to beta testers through **Apple's TestFlight**.

Building a game for a screen this small taught me a lot about game design — the
little things that make a game engaging and fun matter even more when the whole
experience fits on your wrist.

Built with:

- Xcode / Swift
- watchOS 6 independent app SDK
- Beta distribution via TestFlight
